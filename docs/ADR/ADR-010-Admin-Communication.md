# ADR-010: Admin communication with users (Planned)

> Status: Proposed · Last updated: 2026-11-01

## Decision (proposed)

Support and refund conversations happen by e-mail outside the app in Phase 1. Payment decisions are recorded with a note on the payment submission (`approve` / `reject` accept a `note`). No in-app inbox or broadcast tool is built; transactional e-mail provider is TBD (see [Email](../Integrations/50_Email.md)).
