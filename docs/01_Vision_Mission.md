# Vision & Mission

Status: MASTER / CANONICAL · Owner: Ashwin Swaraj · Last updated: 2026-11-01

## Vision

Everyone who earns money, however many places it comes from, can see the full arc of it: what came in, where it went, what they owe, what they have built and what the next festival or function will cost. They get this from a patient accountant they can simply talk to.

## Mission

Give every individual and small-business owner in India a personal accountant that keeps clean books across all their income sources, answers in plain language, and never makes a change they did not agree to.

## Who we serve

- Salaried people with side income or investments.
- Small shop owners and freelancers who mix personal and business money.
- Families who budget around festivals and functions.

## The problem

Money is scattered across cash, UPI, cards, wallets and several businesses. Spreadsheets are tedious, apps assume one income and double-count card bills and transfers, and a human accountant is unaffordable for small amounts.

## Strategic bets

1. **One set of books, many projects.** All movements in one transactions table; a project is a label, so combined reports are always possible.
2. **Chat plus sheets.** The chatbot edits the same Excel-style sheets the user sees, so nothing is hidden.
3. **Trust by design.** Server-enforced confirmation for changes and deletes, an audit log of every change, calculations done by code.
4. **Indian reality.** Rupees in paise, UPI and cash as first-class payment modes, festivals as a section, April–March financial year.

## Principles

- Accuracy over cleverness: numbers are computed, not generated.
- Consent before change: destructive actions need confirmation; deletes are recoverable for 30 days.
- Privacy: users never see each other's rows; the admin panel is reachable only behind an admin check.
- Honesty about scope: Phase 1 is manual entry plus chat; automatic bank and SMS import is Phase 2.

## Success signals

A user keeps a project up to date weekly; asks the chatbot at least a few questions a month; trusts the totals enough to export them (PDF / Excel / CSV / Word); and converts from the free trial to a paid plan.
