# Support & Refund Lifecycle

> Status: Planned · Owner: Ashwin Swaraj · Last updated: 2026-11-01

## Intended flow

`open` -> `in review` -> `resolved` or `rejected`. A refund request links to a payment and, if accepted, ends the subscription early and is repaid manually.

Nothing is implemented: no ticket table, no refund endpoint. The refund policy is TBD (`27_Policies.md`).
