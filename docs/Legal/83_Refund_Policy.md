# Refund Policy

> **Draft — not legal advice, needs lawyer review.** Operator: Wealthspan (legal entity, address and grievance officer: TBD). Last updated: 2026-11-01.

Phase 1 payments are manual UPI payments reviewed by an admin, so refunds are manual too.

- **Free trial:** nothing is charged.
- **Proposed rule (owner to confirm):** a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first paid purchase; after that, no refund for the current period, and you keep access until it ends. Cancelling stops renewal.
- **Wrong or duplicate payment:** refunded in full to the same UPI account once verified by the UTR.
- **Rejected payment submission:** no subscription is created; if money was actually debited, contact support with the UTR.
- **How:** e-mail support (address TBD). Target reply time: TBD. Refunds are sent back by UPI/bank transfer.
- Consumer-law rights that cannot be waived still apply; lawyer to review.
