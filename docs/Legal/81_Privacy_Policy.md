# Privacy Policy

> **Draft — not legal advice, needs lawyer review.** Operator: Wealthspan (legal entity, address and grievance officer: TBD). Last updated: 2026-11-01.

## What we collect

- **Account:** name and e-mail from your sign-in (Google, Zoho or e-mail), handled by Zoho Catalyst Authentication.
- **Your finance records:** projects, accounts, transactions, loans, investments, events, with amounts, dates, categories, notes.
- **Chat messages** you send to the AI accountant.
- **Subscription data:** plan, dates, and the UPI reference (UTR) you submit.
- **Technical data:** server logs (errors, timing); no advertising trackers.

We do **not** collect bank or card credentials, passwords for other apps, or your SMS in Phase 1. Bank/SMS import (Phase 2) will need your separate consent.

## Where it is stored and who processes it

- Records are stored in Zoho Catalyst Data Store, India data centre.
- **Chat text is sent to Sarvam AI** to generate replies. Do not put information in chat you do not want processed there. Your ledger is not sent today.
- Zoho Catalyst (hosting, login) and Sarvam are our processors.

## Why we use it

To run the app, show your reports, produce exports, apply your plan, prevent abuse (rate limits, daily chat quota) and support you. We do not sell your data or use it for ads.

## Who can see it

Only you see your records. Admins can see account and subscription information and payment submissions needed to run the service, and an audit log; access is limited to pinned admin accounts.

## Your rights

Export your data (PDF, Excel, CSV, Word) from the app. Ask us to correct or delete it; account deletion in the app is Planned, until then request it by e-mail (TBD). Deleted records are recoverable for 30 days, then purged ([retention](Data_Retention_Policy.md)). Complaints go to the grievance officer (TBD). Compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 needs lawyer review.

## Security

See the Security docs; in short: login sessions, per-user isolation on every query, request protection, rate limits. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users of a breach as the law requires.

Not intended for under 18 (TBD).
